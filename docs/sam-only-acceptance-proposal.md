## Required forward migration design

The current operation-keyed permits cannot authorize another test without rewriting historical state. Do not rearm them. A new migration must introduce an independent append-only authorization mechanism, reviewed/tested before hosted approval:

- Private RLS-denied `test_authorizations`: immutable UUID, external approval reference, designated owner, operation CHECK segment, model CHECK sam-3.1, concept CHECK chair, exact input SHA, cap 2500, issuance/expiry, revoked timestamp and consumption timestamp/job ID. Retain tombstones without Auth cascade. Unique approval reference prevents reissuance on setup rerun. No browser/worker issuance grants.
- Private one-to-one authorization/job assignment (or immutable authorization FK on jobs) prevents an owner with multiple queued segment jobs from consuming the wrong ticket. Existing first-smoke permits remain byte-for-byte unchanged. Bind admission to the designated owner, operation, concept and ready consented asset digest; client ownership stays server-derived.
- Operator-only issuance locks global/designated budgets in deterministic order. Require the known consumed first-smoke record, reconcile the historical 4402-micro Spark receipt without changing it, reject any unknown dispatch or outstanding reservation/cleanup, reject another issued ticket, require all other accounts to have zero limits/reservations. With historical spend S, temporarily permit only S+2500 at global and designated accounts; preserve all spend counters and credit events. Account for the historical owner's deletion explicitly; do not transfer or erase its charges.
- Admission atomically reserves 2500 against global/designated accounts. Claim uses SKIP LOCKED and verifies the assigned unexpired ticket. Dispatch consumes exactly one ticket under row lock in the same transaction as lease fencing and durable dispatch marker, before any provider transmission. No generic segment permit fallback. Wrong owner/hash/concept/model, cancellation/revocation, second dispatch and concurrent consumers fail closed.
- A post-dispatch crash is uncertain with reservation held and ticket permanently consumed. Reconcile provider receipt; never reset the consumption marker or retry automatically. Cancellation suppresses results and queues physical deletion; it cannot retract upstream processing or charges. Revoke unused ticket and set admission headroom to zero when finished; do not reset accounting.
- Add owner/anonymous/worker privilege tests, immutable/duplicate issuance tests, concurrent admission/dispatch tests, exact-hash/model/operation tests, cancellation/lease expiry tests, crash/uncertain reconciliation and cleanup tests in disposable local PostgreSQL. Migration artifact/checksum, final worker diff and passing CI must be reviewed, then separately approved before applying to cafkyowssdemzivhzxbb. Current worker does not support this design yet.
